# The filed return and the far marks — prose draft (Horizon 9)

**Status: SECOND DRAFT. R1–R4 came back answered and are folded in below; the
legend set grew from four to TEN at the same time.** No code exists yet,
deliberately — the gate that worked for the UNSOUNDED fragments and for
Directive 9. Read it aloud. Veto freely. Three new decisions are marked
**S1–S3**.

**R1 — the two-stage hold.** Adopted as drafted.
**R2 — the sender.** Adopted, and taken further: the channel does not answer
to one name. A small table of sender lines, picked by hash per reply, so the
player never learns who or what is out there listening.
**R3 — the layup.** Adopted, and doubled: **deposit and withdrawal**. The
cache is a two-way place.
**R4 — the Standing Order hails first.** Adopted as drafted.

The register rule (§92) still governs every line: understandable on first
read, eerie by what it means, never by being hard to parse.

---

## A. The verb (commit 0)

Per R1, one mast carries two verbs as two stops in one hold. The first
completion reads the page (3s, as shipped); continuing to hold rolls into the
filing (another 3s, the gauge relabels) — the deeper commitment literally
deeper in the gesture.

The tap, for a pilot who has heard the request and carries unfiled cells:

> SOMEBODY ELSE'S BEACON — A PAGE IS CLIPPED TO THE MAST. HOLD TO READ IT,
> AND KEEP HOLDING TO FILE YOUR RETURN WITH THE NETWORK

The second gauge label, which is the statement of terms (§58) — this is
irreversible and it costs cargo, so the label says so while the bar is still
moving:

> FILING WITH THE NETWORK — NO PAYMENT, NO REGISTRY

Completion:

> RETURN FILED — %d CELLS, PASSED HAND TO HAND. THE HOLD IS LIGHTER.

Refusals stay exact: nothing to file → *"NOTHING NEW TO FILE — THE NETWORK
ALREADY HOLDS YOUR MARKS"*; request not heard → the verb is simply absent, not
locked, because a lock advertises.

## B. The channel answers (commit 0)

Per R2, the sender is never the same twice. One of these, picked by hash on
the filing:

> A CHANNEL, KEPT OPEN — LISTENING
> A CHANNEL, KEPT OPEN — NO CALL SIGN OFFERED
> SOMEBODY'S RELAY — THE HANDSHAKE IS OLD
> A KEPT CHANNEL
> · · · — A CARRIER THAT ANSWERS

The body, on the next outward band crossing after filing, once per batch:

> *Your return is read. It is held, and it is true, and somebody knows it.
> There is a mark at bearing %s, %s out — it is ours, and now it is yours to
> read.*

Captain's log, terse register:

> FILED WITH THE NETWORK — THEIR ANSWER NAMES A MARK, BEARING %03d

## C. The rival, re-read (commit 1)

> A COLLEAGUE WAS FIRST — THE NETWORK HOLDS THEIR MARK, AND YOURS

## D. The marks, and the ten legends

Every mark speaks twice: the buoy's plate when resolved (what the network
sounded here), then the encounter itself. **The marks are honest** — the
network does not lie to its own people — and where there is horror, the horror
is that honesty out here is frightening enough.

The unreadable tap, for a pilot who has never filed:

> A SOUNDING MARK — THE NOTATION IS NOT THE AUTHORITY'S. YOU CANNOT READ IT.

Ten kinds in two classes. **COMMON** kinds may repeat across a run; **SINGULAR**
kinds exist at most ONCE in an entire game, so meeting one is an event a player
will describe to somebody.

### COMMON

#### 1. THE VEIN — *generous, plain*

> MARK: DEEP LODE. THE VEIN RUNS RICHER THAN ANYTHING ON THE CHARTED SIDE AND
> IT HAS NO BOTTOM THAT WE FOUND. BRING A TORCH AND WATCH THE WEATHER.

Band-6 seams at multiples of the best charted yield, in storm-prone open deep.
Not every gift has teeth — **one mark keeping its promise plainly is what makes
every other mark's warning credible.**

#### 2. THE LAYUP — *generous, and two-way (R3)*

> MARK: LAYUP. STORES CACHED BY CREWS WHO DID NOT ALL COME BACK FOR THEM.
> TAKE WHAT YOU NEED. LEAVE WHAT YOU CAN. IT HAS ALWAYS WORKED THAT WAY.

Withdrawal: a large one-time haul of goods and salvage. Deposit: leave cargo,
**for nothing** — no credit, no counter, no return ever. The only purely
expressive verb in the game, and by this arc's own logic the most meaningful
thing in it. The response is one line and no number:

> IT IS IN THE CACHE. SOMEBODY WILL NEED IT. THAT IS THE WHOLE ARRANGEMENT.

**S1 — does a deposit leave a trace?** My lean: the layup's plate, on a later
visit, reads *"STORES CACHED BY CREWS WHO DID NOT ALL COME BACK FOR THEM — AND
BY YOU"*. One clause, no tally.

#### 3. THE PROVING DRIFT — *generous, and the only source of what it carries*

> MARK: PROVING DRIFT. A WORKSHOP HULL. THEY WERE BUILDING PAST THE CATALOGUE
> BECAUSE NOBODY OUT HERE IS SELLING THEM ANYTHING. SOME OF IT WORKS.

A refuser expedition's workshop, cracked like any hull, carrying **prototype
modules that no counter anywhere stocks** — the tier above the Mk V line, one
per drift, and the only way to own one. Not strictly better: prototypes carry a
real defect apiece (heat, draw, a floor), because *"nothing in the catalogue is
a straight upgrade"* is a law here and a legendary is not an exemption.

### SINGULAR — one per game, or none

#### 4. THE STILLNESS' GROUND — *dread*

> MARK: STANDING WARNING. IT PASSES HERE. IF YOUR DRIVES GO QUIET, THEY WILL
> COME BACK. DO NOT BURN. DO NOT TRANSMIT. WAIT.

Drives to zero — a becalming, not damage, not EMP. Something crosses the far
edge of the sensor picture while you drift, a shape the radar refuses to tier.
Then the drives return, exactly as the mark promised. On the far side, a
reading. Ship's voice, once, inside:

> DRIVES QUIET. THE MARK SAID THIS HAPPENS. THE MARK SAID THEY COME BACK.

The Stillness is **NEUTRAL to the surveyor** in the shipped roster, and that is
the strangest fact in this encounter: nothing here is attacking you. It is
simply passing, and you are simply in the way of it.

#### 5. THE STANDING ORDER — *the fight, and it is polite (R4)*

> MARK: HAZARD, PERMANENT. AN AUTHORITY ASSET HOLDS THIS GROUND. IT WAS NOT
> RECALLED. IT CANNOT BE REASONED WITH, BECAUSE NOBODY EVER TOLD IT ANYTHING.
> IF YOU FIGHT IT, FIGHT IT AWAKE.

It hails first, in the warm shipped register:

> **GALACTIC SURVEY AUTHORITY — STATION ASSET** — *Registered vessel. This
> volume is closed to survey by standing directive. Return to the charted side.
> This instruction will be repeated.*

It repeats it. Then it fires. Page 4 of Directive 9 met in person, flying
Authority teal in a place the Authority says does not exist. The kill line is
the arc's thesis, plainly:

> ASSET SILENCED. IT WAS DOING ITS JOB. IT WAS ALWAYS JUST DOING ITS JOB.

#### 6. THE SOUNDING SERPENT — *the thing that is alive*

PYX-03's band-5 broadcast has always said: *"No bottom at this mark. The line
is not short; the bottom is not there."* This is what the line found.

> MARK: DO NOT SOUND HERE. WE PAID OUT THE WHOLE LINE AND SOMETHING TOOK THE
> WEIGHT OFF THE END OF IT. IT IS LONG. IT IS NOT A MACHINE.

Everything in this game is machinery and residue. A **living** thing is the
one genuine escalation left, and it must be unmistakably alive: it does not
strafe, it *swims* — a segmented body that moves through the play plane and
takes damage per segment, so the fight is about where you are rather than how
much you shoot. It has no faction, no flag, no wreck, and it does not drop
salvage. What it drops is that you saw it.

> IT IS GONE. NOTHING ON THE BOARD. NOTHING TO FILE. WE SAW IT.

**S2 — does the serpent stay dead?** My lean: no. It leaves, and the mark
stays live. A creature you exterminated is a boss; a creature that swam off is
a place you now avoid, which is worth more.

#### 7. THE ORRERY STILL RUNNING — *the machines were never told, as a windfall*

> MARK: AN INSTRUMENT NOBODY SWITCHED OFF. IT HAS BEEN SURVEYING THIS VOLUME
> SINCE BEFORE THE ORDER. IT HAS FILED EVERY CYCLE. NOTHING HAS EVER COLLECTED.

A GSA survey instrument, still working, holding forty years of charts nobody
came for. Hold to collect: **the only place in the game charts arrive without
being surveyed**, at a scale a pilot cannot earn in a session. And the
instrument thanks you in the register that is now unbearable:

> COLLECTION ACKNOWLEDGED. YOUR CARRIER IS LOGGED AS THE COLLECTING VESSEL.
> THE SURVEY THANKS YOU FOR YOUR CONTRIBUTION.

That last sentence is Directive 9's own closing line, spoken by a machine that
means it.

#### 8. THE TALLY'S NURSERY — *the hive, and it can be taken*

The Tally is already in the roster: HOSTILE to every chart power, and its
written character is that it **counts what crosses its ground.**

> MARK: THEIR GROUND. HULLS WE DO NOT HAVE NAMES FOR, IN NUMBERS. THEY DO NOT
> RAID AND THEY DO NOT TRADE. THEY COUNT. WE HAVE NEVER SEEN ONE LOSE ANYTHING
> IT WAS HOLDING.

A colony of unfamiliar hulls — silhouettes and weapon families the player has
never met, because they were never in the catalogue. It can be **taken**, and
the mark says plainly that it never has been:

> IF YOU MEAN TO TAKE IT: NOBODY HAS. THAT IS NOT DISCOURAGEMENT, IT IS THE
> RECORD.

Held, it becomes a foothold on the existing colony machinery — a jump anchor at
a depth nothing else reaches. And **they come back for it**, on a clock, in
increasing numbers, forever. It is the only thing in Pyxis you can own and then
have to keep.

#### 9. THE PATTERN FORGE — *the escorts*

> MARK: A FORGE THAT COPIES. FEED IT A HULL AND IT PRINTS THE PATTERN. IT DOES
> NOT ASK WHOSE HULL. WE DID NOT BUILD IT AND WE DO NOT KNOW WHO DID.

Bring it a hull — a wreck, a killed pattern — and it prints escorts of that
pattern that fly with you. **You cannot command them.** They hold formation,
they fire when you are fired on, they die and do not come back. That is the
whole contract, and the refusal is stated up front so nobody expects an RTS:

> THEY WILL NOT TAKE ORDERS. THEY WILL NOT TAKE A BEARING. THEY FOLLOW, AND
> WHEN SOMETHING SHOOTS AT YOU THEY ANSWER. THAT IS ALL IT PRINTS.

**S3 — do escorts survive a warp?** My lean: no — they hold to the system they
were printed in. An escort wing that follows you home makes the charted side
easy, and the charted side must stay exactly as hard as it is.

#### 10. THE PROCESSION'S END — *no reward at all*

> MARK: THEY ARE ALL POINTED HOME. WE COUNTED FOUR HUNDRED AND STOPPED
> COUNTING. IF YOU READ THE HULLS, READ THEM SLOWLY.

A drifting formation of dead ships in perfect order, every one facing the
charted side. Nothing to crack, nothing to mine, nothing to fight. You can
read hulls: each carries a name, and the names are the list from page 6 of
Directive 9 — *"nine names. I have met two of them."* Here are the rest.

The only thing you can do is the layup's verb, once: leave something.

> NOTHING HERE IS SALVAGE. THE HULLS ARE FULL AND NOBODY HAS TOUCHED THEM.

**This mark pays nothing, and that is deliberate.** In a set of ten legends
where nine of them give, one that only asks is what keeps the others from
reading as a loot table.

## What is deliberately not written

- Any anatomy: not the Stillness, not the serpent, not the Tally, not whoever
  built the forge. Marks say *that*, never *why*.
- Any name the network speaks for itself (R2's whole point).
- Any line where the Authority notices. The Standing Order does not report the
  fight. Nobody comes.
- Any counter or checklist for the marks, the legends, or the names.
